- **Employees:** 67 (11.4% YoY growth)

- **Founded:** 2009

- **Website:** grandbeachhotel.com
- **Contact LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/sharley-faria-60281912
- **Services:** Luxury oceanfront accommodations, wellness, fitness, event facilities
- **Why Cane fits:** Largest prospect by headcount.
